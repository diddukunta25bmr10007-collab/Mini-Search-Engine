Good health depends on a balance of diet, exercise, sleep, and stress management.
Regular exercise strengthens the heart and improves overall fitness.
A balanced diet provides the nutrients the body needs to function well.
Getting enough sleep each night is important for both physical and mental health.
Drinking enough water throughout the day supports many body functions.
Mental health is just as important as physical health for overall wellbeing.
Routine checkups with a doctor can catch health issues early.
Strength training and cardio exercise both play a role in fitness.
Stress management techniques like meditation can improve long term health.
Small daily habits often have a bigger impact on health than occasional big changes.
