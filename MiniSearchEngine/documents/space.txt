Space exploration is the study and discovery of outer space using science and technology.
NASA and ISRO are two well known space agencies conducting missions.
The International Space Station orbits Earth and houses astronauts from many countries.
Mars is a major target for future human exploration missions.
Rockets use powerful engines to escape Earth's gravity and reach orbit.
Satellites orbiting Earth are used for communication, weather, and navigation.
The Moon landing in 1969 was a historic milestone in space exploration.
Space telescopes like Hubble allow scientists to observe distant galaxies.
Private companies are now building reusable rockets to reduce launch costs.
Astronauts must train for years before going on a space mission.
