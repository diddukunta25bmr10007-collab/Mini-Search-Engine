The stock market allows people to buy and sell shares of companies.
Stock prices go up and down based on supply and demand.
Investors track the market using indexes like the Nasdaq and S&P 500.
A bull market means prices are generally rising.
A bear market means prices are generally falling.
Traders often use charts and data to predict market movement.
The stock market can be affected by news, earnings, and global events.
Diversifying investments helps reduce risk in the market.
Many people invest in the market for long term retirement savings.
Market volatility can create both risk and opportunity for traders.
