Finance is the study of how people and businesses manage money.
Cryptocurrency is a form of digital money secured by cryptography.
Ethereum is another popular cryptocurrency besides bitcoin.
Financial markets connect buyers and sellers of assets like stocks and crypto.
Banks and financial institutions help manage savings, loans, and investments.
Personal finance includes budgeting, saving, and planning for the future.
Cryptocurrency trading happens on exchanges that operate around the clock.
Interest rates set by central banks influence the wider economy and market.
Understanding finance helps people make smarter decisions with their money.
Some companies now accept cryptocurrency like bitcoin as payment.
