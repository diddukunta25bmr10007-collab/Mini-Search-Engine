Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency created in 2009.
Bitcoin transactions are recorded on a public ledger called the blockchain.
The total supply of bitcoin is capped at 21 million coins.
Bitcoin mining uses computing power to validate transactions.
Many investors treat bitcoin as a store of value like digital gold.
Bitcoin price is known for being extremely volatile.
Wallets are used to store and manage bitcoin securely.
Bitcoin was created by an anonymous person or group called Satoshi Nakamoto.
Exchanges allow users to buy and sell bitcoin for regular currency.
Bitcoin popularized the idea of cryptocurrency worldwide.
