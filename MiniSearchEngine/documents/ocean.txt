The ocean covers more than seventy percent of the Earth's surface.
Ocean currents help regulate the planet's climate and weather patterns.
Marine biologists study ocean life, from tiny plankton to giant whales.
The deepest part of the ocean is the Mariana Trench in the Pacific.
Coral reefs are home to a huge variety of ocean species.
Plastic pollution is a growing threat to ocean ecosystems worldwide.
Ships and submarines allow humans to travel through and explore the ocean.
Tides in the ocean are caused mainly by the gravitational pull of the moon.
Ocean exploration remains limited, with much of the deep sea still unmapped.
Fishing and shipping industries rely heavily on healthy ocean resources.
